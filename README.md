# On Time, On Point — Delivery Performance Analysis


A data analytics capstone project using the public Brazilian E-Commerce dataset by Olist to explore delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

## Project Goal
Analyze delivery delays and explore how they relate to customer review scores.

## Key Findings
- **93.2%** of 96,476 delivered orders arrived on time or early.
- **6.8%** of delivered orders were late.
- Delivery delay and review score had a negative correlation (**-0.267**).
- **Alagoas (AL)** had the highest late-delivery rate (**21.4%**) among states with at least 100 delivered orders.
- The top 10 sellers accounted for about **16%** of late order-seller pairs.

These results show an association between delays and lower review scores, but do not prove that delays caused lower scores.

## Dataset
The project uses the [Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce).

Download the dataset from Kaggle. No private or restricted information is used.

## Analysis
The project looks at:
- Late-delivery rates by customer state
- Delivery delays and review scores
- Delivery performance by product category
- Late deliveries by seller
- Monthly delivery trends

## Recommendations
- Investigate states with high late-delivery rates.
- Monitor delivery performance alongside customer satisfaction.
- Review seller-level delays to identify where support or investigation may be needed.

## Limitations
- The dataset is historical and observational.
- Review scores reflect overall order satisfaction, not delivery alone.
- Results may vary by location, product category, seller, and order volume.
- Correlation does not prove causation.
